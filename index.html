FAQs

Before
we start.

Is $100 the total cost?

No. It covers your first month of management. Your Google advertising budget is separate, paid directly to Google. Management is $249/month from your second month.

How much should I spend on ads?

That depends on your location, competition, and goals. We’ll help you choose a starting budget for your business.

When will I start getting inquiries?

Ads can appear after approval and campaign launch. Results depend on your market, budget, and other factors. Leads and bookings are not guaranteed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There’s no commitment, and you can cancel anytime.

Do I need a website?

We recommend a website where people can learn about your services and contact you. Website development, website changes, and landing pages are not included. We can review existing conversion tracking and coordinate with your website provider if changes are needed.

Already running Google Ads?

We can review your existing Google Search campaigns and manage them moving forward.
