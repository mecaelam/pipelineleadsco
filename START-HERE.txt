PREVIEW
Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.

UPLOAD TO YOUR OWN HOST
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, hero-search.js, pricing.js, process-route.js, navigation.js, and the assets folder together to your host's public web folder (often public_html, www, or htdocs).
Keep index.html at the public folder's root. No build command, package installation, ChatGPT account, or ChatGPT hosting is needed.

YOUR DOMAIN
Use the DNS records supplied by your chosen hosting provider to connect your domain. Enable HTTPS through your host. Exact DNS records depend on the host you choose.
