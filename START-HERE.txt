UPLOAD TO YOUR OWN HOST
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, hero-search.js, pricing.js, and the assets folder together to your host's public web folder (often public_html, www, or htdocs).

YOUR DOMAIN
Use the DNS records supplied by your chosen hosting provider to connect your domain. Enable HTTPS through your host. Exact DNS records depend on the host you choose.
